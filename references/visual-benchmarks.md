# Reference Images Structural DNA (`visual-benchmarks.md`)

Abstracted visual mechanics extracted from high-performing conceptual Instagram grids. Apply these structural patterns to any product category without copying literal props.

---

## 1. Spatial & Scale Variance
- **Macro Bleed (110%–130%):** Hero object is cropped at the frame edges (top/bottom or single side) to force tactile texture inspection, leaving minimal background exposure.
- **Standard Anchor (40%–55%):** Centered or slightly off-axis medium shot acting as the grid’s visual baseline (**use at most once per grid**, strictly avoiding flat tabletop clichés; must be suspended, contextual, or off-table); balanced against 45%–60% negative space for primary headlines.
- **Micro Isolation (12%–15%, max 20% in secondary containers):** Hero object is shrunk and housed inside a secondary vessel, architectural niche, or surrounded by 80%–85%+ uninterrupted color fields to force immediate focal-point hunting (fulfilling the grid's requirement of at least one tile with <15% product scale in vast negative space).
- **Multiplication / Crowd Density (70%–90%):** Single hero unit is multiplied into a dense, overlapping cluster, repeating pattern, or geometric grid (e.g., 3x3 or tic-tac-toe matrix) floating or arranged in geometric space (never resting on a flat tabletop), shifting focus from individual form to collective abundance.
- **Asymmetric Split (50/50):** Hero object is pushed strictly to one vertical half or diagonal corner, dedicating the remaining half to high-density typographic blocks.

---

## 2. Physics & Metaphor
- **Zero-Gravity Suspension:** Hero object floats mid-air with cast drop-shadows beneath it, detached from any surface plane.
- **Scale Inversion & Containment:** Hero object is miniaturized to fit inside an incongruous luxury or industrial vessel (e.g., a jewelry box, wall-mounted emergency display case, or human silhouette profile).
- **Hybrid Object Replacement:** Hero object physically replaces a functional component of an unrelated machine or vehicle (e.g., acting as a rocket fuselage, helmet visor, parachute canopy, or fuel gauge dial).
- **Screen-Boundary Breach (2.5D Extrusion):** Hero object breaks out of a 2D UI frame, smartphone screen, social media card, or printed newspaper page into the foreground plane.
- **Kinetic Action Freeze:** Hero object is captured mid-pour, mid-lift, or surrounded by suspended radial debris/particles radiating outward from the center of mass.

---

## 3. Typography Geometry
- **Z-Axis Layer Interlock:** Oversized, high-weight display type is stacked vertically in the background; the hero object is layered over the middle letterforms, forcing the viewer to mentally complete occluded characters.
- **Split-Word & Glyph Substitution:** Headlines are broken across multiple lines with exaggerated kerning, or individual letters (typically "O" or "0") are replaced entirely by top-down circular hero objects.
- **Radial & Halo Paths:** Text is set along a circular or elliptical path orbiting the hero object like a halo or mechanical claw boundary.
- **Kinetic Ribbon / Ticker Tape:** Repeating micro-copy runs along angled diagonal bands or horizontal strips that physically wrap behind and in front of the hero object.
- **UI & System Mimicry:** Copy is housed inside recognizable digital interface containers—OS alert modals, chat bubbles, toggle switches, or pill-shaped CTA buttons—anchoring the bottom margin.
- **Typographic Repetition Walls:** A single word is repeated in a rigid vertical or horizontal matrix behind the hero object, creating a textured wallpaper pattern rather than a traditional headline.

---

## 4. Color & Contrast Rhythm (Harmonized with Strict 2–3 Palette)
- **Checkerboard Alternation:** High-saturation dark or primary-colored tiles alternate strictly with off-white, cream, or pale-neutral tiles across the grid matrix, preventing adjacent visual clutter.
- **Monochromatic Saturation Blocks:** Entire tiles are flooded with a single dominant hue from the locked 2–3 color palette (background, prop, and shadow tinted identically) so the hero object provides the sole tonal break, functioning as the dark/saturated tile in the checkerboard rhythm.
- **Inverted Duotone Anchors:** A strict 2-to-3 color palette is inverted from tile to tile (e.g., dark background/light type vs. light background/dark type) while keeping corner badges and footer bars locked in fixed screen coordinates.
- **Neutral Field + Pop Accent:** 80% warm off-white or muted neutral background from the locked 2–3 color palette paired with a single high-contrast geometric shape (starburst, circle, or grid) in the accent color directly behind the hero object to create artificial depth, functioning as the light/clean tile in the checkerboard rhythm.

---

## 5. Complimentary Elements
- **1-Bit / Monoline Doodle Overlays:** Hand-drawn white or dark vector outlines (arrows, stars, spirals, speed lines, and framing brackets) are drawn directly over photographic surfaces to indicate motion, heat, or focal direction.
- **Diegetic UI & Gamification:** Interactive visual tropes—checklists, star ratings, heart/bookmark icons, loyalty punch-cards, and sliding toggles—frame the hero object as an actionable digital choice.
- **Architectural & Geometric Framing:** Hard-edged vector shapes (checkerboard strips, wavy ribbons, Venn diagram rings, or recessed 3D shadow boxes) isolate the hero object from the background plane.
- **Tactile Paper & Print Artifacts:** Ripped paper edges, halftone newsprint textures, binder clips, and vertical micro-labels (serial numbers, timestamps, and corner stamps) impose an editorial magazine structure over commercial photography.
- **Human Interaction Cropping:** Hands enter from the frame edges without visible faces—pinching, holding, lifting, or gesturing toward the hero object—to establish scale and immediate physical utility.
