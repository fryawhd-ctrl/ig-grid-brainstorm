# Typography Pairings Library for Instagram Grids

Consistent typography is critical for holding a 6-tile grid together. When viewer eyes scan across 2 rows of 3 columns, consistent typeface families, weight hierarchies, and tracking create instant brand recognition.

---

## Curated Font Pairings by Aesthetic Mood

All pairings utilize freely accessible Google Fonts and standard Canva/Figma fonts.

### 1. Warm Editorial / Lifestyle & Hospitality
- **Headline Font**: **Playfair Display** or **Cinzel** (Weight: 600 Semi-Bold / Italic accents)
- **Body / Subtext Font**: **Plus Jakarta Sans** or **Inter** (Weight: 400 Regular / 500 Medium)
- **Tracking / Letter Spacing**:
  - Headlines: Normal or slight negative (`-0.5px`)
  - Subheaders / Labels: Wide uppercase tracking (`+1.5px` to `+2.0px`)
- **Best For**: Specialty coffee, artisanal products, interior design, slow living, boutique hotels.

---

### 2. Modern Clean Minimalist / Tech & SaaS
- **Headline Font**: **Syne** or **Outfit** (Weight: 700 Bold or 800 Extra-Bold)
- **Body / Subtext Font**: **DM Sans** or **Inter** (Weight: 400 Regular)
- **Monospace Accent / Data Tag**: **Space Mono** or **JetBrains Mono**
- **Tracking / Letter Spacing**:
  - Headlines: Tight (`-1px`)
  - Accent Tags: Uppercase with wide tracking (`+1.5px`)
- **Best For**: Digital creators, apps, web design, productivity, modern workspace.

---

### 3. Bold Neo-Brutalist / Streetwear & Gen-Z
- **Headline Font**: **Archivo Black** or **Clash Display** (Weight: 900 Black)
- **Body / Subtext Font**: **Space Grotesk** or **Work Sans** (Weight: 500 Medium)
- **Tracking / Letter Spacing**:
  - Headlines: Very tight (`-2px`), high contrast, solid block containers
- **Best For**: Creative agencies, streetwear brands, events, edgy personal brands.

### 4. Elegant Luxury / High-End Fashion & Jewelry
- **Headline Font**: **Cormorant Garamond** or **Bodoni Moda** (Weight: 400 Light / 500 Medium)
- **Body / Subtext Font**: **Montserrat** or **Tenor Sans** (Weight: 300 Light / 400 Regular)
- **Tracking / Letter Spacing**:
  - Headlines: Generous letter-spacing (`+1.0px`)
  - Subheaders: Uppercase with wide tracking (`+2.5px`)
- **Best For**: High jewelry, luxury skincare, high fashion, architectural portfolios.

### 5. Organic Earthy / Wellness & Sustainability

## Hierarchy & Layout Rules for On-Image Text

1. **The 3-Second Rule**: Viewers scrolling the feed spend under 3 seconds per tile. Overlay text must never exceed 8-10 words on a single tile.
2. **Text Contrast Guarantee**: Always ensure overlay text sits on a solid color block, a subtle gradient scrim, or an area of high negative space. Minimum contrast ratio: `4.5:1`.
3. **Consistent Coordinate Badging**: If numbering or badging tiles (e.g. `[01/06]`), keep the badge font, size, and position identical across all 6 tiles (e.g., top-left corner in secondary font, uppercase, `+2px` tracking).
