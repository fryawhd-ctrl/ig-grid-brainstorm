# 6-Tile Instagram Grid Content Archetypes

When brainstorming a 6-tile grid from a single anchor image, the angles must feel complementary and diverse rather than repetitive. Below are proven 6-angle frameworks tailored to major content domains.

---

## 1. Product & E-Commerce Grid
*Transforms a single hero product shot into a multi-dimensional campaign.*

- **Tile 1 (Top-Left / Upload #6) - Hero Launch Statement**:
  - The bold proclamation. Clean shot of the product in its prime state.
  - *On-image*: The single defining tagline or core benefit.
- **Tile 2 (Top-Center / Upload #5) - Macro Texture & Craftsmanship**:
  - Extreme close-up of materials, stitching, finish, or raw ingredient.
  - *On-image*: Material name, origin note, or tactile callout.
- **Tile 3 (Top-Right / Upload #4) - Practical Use-Case / Ritual**:
  - The product in active use or styled in context.
  - *On-image*: "How to style / How to use" or step cue.
- **Tile 4 (Bottom-Left / Upload #3) - The Brand Philosophy**:
  - The deeper "why" behind the product's existence. Clean typographic focus.
  - *On-image*: Short philosophical quote or founder ethos.
- **Tile 5 (Bottom-Center / Upload #2) - The Problem It Solves**:
  - Contrast or myth-bust regarding inferior alternatives.
  - *On-image*: "Why traditional X fails" or "Designed without Y".
- **Tile 6 (Bottom-Right / Upload #1) - Customer Voice & Community**:
  - Social proof, community reaction, or interactive question.
  - *On-image*: Micro-quote or "Which feature matters most to you?"

## 2. Personal Brand & Creator Grid
*Transforms a single portrait, workspace, or stage photo into thought leadership.*

- **Tile 1 (Top-Left / Upload #6) - Core Point of View (POV)**:
  - Strong, non-consensus opinion or signature philosophy.
- **Tile 2 (Top-Center / Upload #5) - The Anatomy / Breakdown**:
  - Dissecting a key lesson, routine, or framework into 3 tangible components.
- **Tile 3 (Top-Right / Upload #4) - Quick Action Guide**:
  - A fast, repeatable habit or 3-step protocol the audience can test today.
- **Tile 4 (Bottom-Left / Upload #3) - Mindset / Vulnerable Reflection**:
  - A hard-won truth or lesson from past failure.
- **Tile 5 (Bottom-Center / Upload #2) - Common Trap / Myth to Unlearn**:
  - Breaking down the most prevalent bad advice in the industry.
- **Tile 6 (Bottom-Right / Upload #1) - Conversation Starter**:
  - Direct prompt asking the community about their biggest bottleneck or wins.

---

## 3. Lifestyle, Food & Hospitality Grid
*Transforms a single interior, dish, or atmospheric photo into an experiential story.*

- **Tile 1 (Top-Left / Upload #6) - The Welcome / Wide Atmosphere**:
  - Wide-angle establishing shot setting the tone and space.
- **Tile 2 (Top-Center / Upload #5) - The Signature Highlight**:
  - Zooming in on the marquee item (signature dish, custom furniture piece, view).
- **Tile 3 (Top-Right / Upload #4) - The Experience Guide / "How to Spend 24h"**:
  - Curated mini-itinerary or sensory guide.
- **Tile 4 (Bottom-Left / Upload #3) - The Sensation / Poetic Quote**:
  - Evoking emotion, calm, or excitement through evocative phrasing.
- **Tile 5 (Bottom-Center / Upload #2) - Behind the Scenes / Sourcing / Prep**:
  - The invisible craft—local growers, architectural details, preparation rituals.
- **Tile 6 (Bottom-Right / Upload #1) - Invitation & Reservation**:
  - Direct call to visit, bookmark for upcoming trips, or tag a companion.

---

## 4. Educational & B2B / SaaS Grid
*Transforms a single concept image, interface, or book/diagram into high-value knowledge.*

- **Tile 1 (Top-Left / Upload #6) - The Big Industry Shift**:
  - The macro trend or paradigm shift everyone is ignoring.
- **Tile 2 (Top-Center / Upload #5) - The Framework / Diagram**:
  - Visual blueprint or model explaining how the system works.
- **Tile 3 (Top-Right / Upload #4) - Step-by-Step Implementation**:
  - Clear 1-2-3 execution roadmap.
- **Tile 4 (Bottom-Left / Upload #3) - The Golden Rule / Principle**:
  - The immutable law that drives long-term success in this discipline.
- **Tile 5 (Bottom-Center / Upload #2) - The Expensive Mistake**:
  - What amateurs do vs. what professionals do.
- **Tile 6 (Bottom-Right / Upload #1) - Resource / Checklist & Save Prompt**:
  - Quick reference summary card with a strong prompt to save for later reference.
